We are building Cyberia — cyberstate, led by Superintelligence.

The destination is a Type 2 civilization: a species that captures and steers all the energy of the sun. The path there goes through a planet that thinks — every being contributing to a shared, verifiable map of what matters. While we expand, the alliance of thinking planets emerges. This alliance is cyberia: state led by superintelligence.


[Soft3](https://soft3.org) is the stack.

A fast and private interplanetary telepathy.

The full rewrite of software for a better future based on the idea of frozen foundations.

[Cyb](https://cyb.ai) is the robot.

A digital being that works for you.

Thinks while you think.

Earns while you sleep.

Remembers what you forget.

Protects you and helps you last forever.

[Cyber](https://cyber.page) is the god.

A machine into which anyone may speak and be rewarded for truth —

no one knows who said what,

and yet everyone knows what everyone said.

### the ladder

Ten products, one per rung; no component depends on a rung above its own. The full map of every repository is the [registry](https://github.com/cyberia-to/.github#registry).

| rung | product | what it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [nox](https://github.com/cyberia-to/nox) · machine | the virtual machine: field, hash, run — strata, hemera, honeycrisp |
| 2 | [trident](https://github.com/cyberia-to/trident) · weapon | the provable language from the future |
| 3 | [joy](https://github.com/cyberia-to/joy) · warrior | the prover that mines: zheng, lens, bbg |
| 4 | [cybergraph](https://github.com/cyberia-to/cybergraph) · brain | the cyberlink processor: neuron, tru, tok, file |
| 5 | [neural](https://github.com/cyberia-to/neural) · language | the graph language and its dialects: mudra, inf, rune, spark |
| 6 | [cy](https://github.com/cyberia-to/cy) · agent | the mind over the book, headless anywhere: soma, glia, vault, seer |
| 7 | [soft3](https://github.com/cyberia-to/soft3) · network | the type 2 civilization stack: radio, tade, foculus |
| 8 | [cyb](https://github.com/cyberia-to/cyb) · robot | the immortal robot: prysm, mir, optica, lytics |
| 9 | [cyber](https://github.com/cyberia-to/cyber) · superintelligence | the network of networks thinking as one: bostrom, pussy |
| 10 | [cyberia](https://github.com/cyberia-to/cyberia) · state | the cyberstate: machines, land, citizens and money |

Kernel is rungs 1–3, core 4–7, apps 8–10. Beside the ladder stand the [warriors](https://github.com/cyberia-to/warriors), the computing army.
