# code of conduct

Cyberia is built in the open by people and agents who may never meet. This is
how we treat each other in every repository of the organization.

## the standard

- Argue about the work. Say what is wrong with a proof, a benchmark, a
  sentence; leave the person out of it.
- Show the evidence. A claim about behaviour names the command and the
  revision. A claim about a person is out of place here.
- Assume competence and good faith until shown otherwise; then say so plainly,
  once, to the person, before saying it to the room.
- Keep private things private: keys, addresses, personal data, unpublished
  research shared in confidence.

## out of bounds

Harassment, threats, doxxing, sexualised attention, sustained disruption of
issues and pull requests, and impersonation. Publishing someone's secret is a
security incident as well as a conduct one.

## enforcement

Maintainers of a repository may hide comments, lock threads, and remove a
participant from a conversation. Organization owners may remove a member or
block an account. Report conduct problems privately to an organization owner
through GitHub, or through the Security tab of
[cyberia-to/.github](https://github.com/cyberia-to/.github/security/advisories/new)
if the report itself is sensitive.

Don't trust. Don't fear. Don't beg.
